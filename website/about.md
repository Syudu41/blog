## From an idea to an explanation

I use this space to slow down and work through the details. An equation, a calculation, and a picture can each reveal something different; bringing them together is how I try to make an explanation useful.

The visualization pages pair animations with written explanations, so you can follow the pictures and return to the mathematics at your own pace.
